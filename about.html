Why we exist

A slower, kinder way to shop

Wedding lehengas worn once. Heirloom jhumkas sitting in a drawer. Handbags bought for an occasion that came and went. Indian wardrobes are full of beautiful things that spend most of their lives unseen.

CIRCLO exists to give those pieces another life. We bring together women who want to pass something on and women who would love to wear it, and we handle the difficult parts in between.

Unlike open listing sites, CIRCLO is curated. A person reviews every submission, so the catalogue stays focused, honest and worth browsing. We would rather list fewer pieces than list the wrong ones.

Because we coordinate every sale and handle collection and delivery ourselves, buyers and sellers never need to share personal contact details with each other.

What we hold to

Our principles

I

Honest listings

Descriptions should say what a piece is, including its flaws. We ask sellers for clear photos and may request changes before approval.

II

Privacy first

Contact details are used only to coordinate a sale and are never published on the website.

III

Personal coordination

A real person confirms each order with both sides before anything moves.

IV

Care in handling

Our team collects each sold piece, checks it against the listing, and delivers it by hand.

The journey of a piece

From one wardrobe to the next

  1. Submitted

    The seller shares photos and details. The piece is saved privately, pending review.

  2. Reviewed

    Our team approves it, asks for changes, or declines it. Only approved pieces appear in the shop.

  3. Enquired & confirmed

    A buyer enquires, and we confirm the order with buyer and seller. The piece is then shown as reserved.

  4. Collected & checked

    We collect the piece from the seller at an agreed time and check it against its listing.

  5. Delivered

    We deliver it to the buyer and record the handover. The piece is marked sold.
