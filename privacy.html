Who we are

CIRCLO is a curated marketplace for women's pre-owned fashion and jewellery. In this policy, “we” means the CIRCLO business that operates this website. Contact details are on our contact page.

What we collect

WhenWhat
You submit a pieceYour name, phone number or email, the item's title, description, category, condition, brand, expected price and the photos you upload.
You send an enquiryYour name, phone number or email, the piece you asked about and your message.
You send a contact messageYour name, phone number or email, subject and message.
An order is arrangedPickup and delivery addresses, dates, time slots, the team member assigned and the name of the person who received the item.

The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our team sign-in stores a temporary session token in your browser tab only while a team member is signed in.

How we use it

  • To review and, if approved, list a submitted piece.
  • To respond to enquiries and messages.
  • To confirm orders and arrange pickup and delivery.
  • To keep an internal record of changes made by our team.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your separate consent.

What is shown publicly

Only approved listings are shown: title, description, category, condition, brand, price, availability and photos. Seller and buyer names and contact details are never shown on the website.

Where it is stored

Information is stored in a private Google Sheet and photos in a private Google Drive folder owned by CIRCLO, accessed through Google Apps Script. Photos are made viewable by link only while a listing is public; photos of pending, rejected, sold or hidden pieces are kept private.

How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as needed to complete a sale and for our business records. You can ask us to delete your information where we are not required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw a submission, by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
