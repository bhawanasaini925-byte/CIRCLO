Questions

Good to know

How do I buy something?

Open a piece and send an enquiry. We'll contact you to confirm availability, price and delivery. Once confirmed, we collect the piece from the seller and deliver it to you.

Can I pay online?

Not on the website. Payment is agreed directly with CIRCLO when your order is confirmed.

Is everything I submit published?

No. Every submission is reviewed first. We may approve it, ask for changes, or decline it.
