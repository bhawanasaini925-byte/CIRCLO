Frequently asked questions

Buying

How do I buy a piece?

Open the piece and choose “Enquire about this piece”. We'll contact you to confirm availability, the final price and a delivery slot. Your order is confirmed only after we've spoken with you.

Can I pay on the website?

No. There is no online checkout. Payment terms are agreed with CIRCLO when we confirm your order.

What does “Reserved” mean?

Another buyer's order has been confirmed for that piece. If their order is cancelled, the piece becomes available again.

Can I see the piece before buying?

Ask in your enquiry. Viewing arrangements depend on the piece and location and aren't guaranteed.

Selling

What can I sell?

Women's sarees, lehengas, kurtis, dresses, ethnic wear, handbags, clutches, accessories and jewellery in good, wearable condition.

Is my submission published straight away?

No. Every submission is saved privately as pending review. We'll approve it, ask for changes, or decline it, and contact you either way.

Who sets the price?

You suggest an expected price. We may discuss it with you during review. The listed price is agreed before publishing.

Will buyers see my phone number?

No. Seller contact details are never shown on the website. CIRCLO coordinates every sale.

Pickup & delivery

Who collects and delivers?

CIRCLO's own team, within our service area. We don't use an external courier at the moment.

How is pickup arranged?

Once an order is confirmed, we agree a date and time slot with the seller and collect the piece in person.

Do you check the piece?

Yes. After collection we check it against its listing before scheduling delivery. If it doesn't match, we'll contact both parties before going ahead.

What if I miss a pickup or delivery?

We'll contact you to reschedule. Repeated missed slots may lead to the order being cancelled.

Cancellations

Can I cancel an order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. Orders can be cancelled before delivery; the piece then returns to the shop. Specific cancellation terms are set out in our terms and conditions.

Can a seller withdraw a piece?

Yes, before an order is confirmed. Contact us and we'll hide the listing.

What happens if a delivery fails?

We'll try to reschedule. If delivery can't be completed, the piece may be returned to the seller and the order cancelled.

Item condition

What do the condition labels mean?

New with tags: unworn with original tags. Like new: worn once or twice, no visible wear. Gently used: light signs of wear. Well loved: visible wear, described in the listing.

Are items authenticated?

We review photos and descriptions and check items at collection, but we don't provide formal authentication or certification for brands or jewellery metals.

Are pre-owned items cleaned?

Unless a listing says otherwise, pieces are delivered as they were collected. Ask in your enquiry if this matters to you.
