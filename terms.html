About these terms

These terms apply to anyone using the CIRCLO website to browse, enquire about, buy or submit pre-owned items. By using the website you agree to them.

Our service

CIRCLO is a managed marketplace. We review submitted items, publish approved listings, coordinate between buyers and sellers, and arrange local pickup and delivery with our own team. CIRCLO does not offer online payment through this website.

Seller responsibilities

  • You must own the item and have the right to sell it.
  • Your description and photos must be accurate and disclose known flaws.
  • Submitting an item does not guarantee approval. We may approve, request changes, or decline at our discretion.
  • Once an order is confirmed, you agree to make the item available for collection at the agreed time.
  • Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe or prohibited items must not be submitted.

Buyer responsibilities

  • An enquiry is not an order. An order exists only once CIRCLO confirms it with you.
  • You agree to be available at the agreed delivery time and to confirm receipt.
  • Pre-owned items may show signs of wear consistent with their stated condition.

Item condition

Condition labels are a guide. We review listings and check items at collection, but we do not provide formal authentication, certification or guarantees of brand, material or metal purity unless explicitly stated in a listing.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup and delivery are carried out by CIRCLO within our service area on agreed dates and time slots. Times are estimates. If a pickup or delivery fails, we will try to reschedule. Repeated failures may lead to cancellation.

Cancellations and returns

Orders may be cancelled by either party before delivery by contacting CIRCLO. If an item does not match its listing at collection, we may cancel the order. Return terms after delivery are agreed case by case. The business owner should define a specific return policy before launch.

Liability

CIRCLO takes reasonable care when handling items. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the agreed price of the item concerned.
